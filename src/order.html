How to Order

Your favorite campus meal is just four simple steps away.

ORDER PROCESS

Four Easy Steps

01

Choose Your Food

Explore our menu and decide what you would like to eat.

02

Visit the Counter

Tell our cafeteria staff your selected items and quantity.

03

Pay & Collect Token

Pay at the counter and keep your order token safely.

04

Collect Your Order

Wait for your token number and collect your prepared meal.

Peak Hours

The cafeteria may be busier during lunch breaks. Please allow extra time during peak hours.

Pickup Guidelines

Keep your token until collection. Check your order before leaving the pickup counter.

Need Assistance?

Contact our cafeteria staff if your token is delayed or an item is missing.
